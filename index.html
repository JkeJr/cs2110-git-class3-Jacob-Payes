My Favorite Technology

It's hard to say what "technology" is my favorite, but I am consistently fascinated with Cathode Ray Tube (CRT) TVs. These are the TVs I grew up on, and even though modern LCD, OLED, and other forms of screen are more efficient, I love how much technology went into these CRT displays. Of course, they function very differently from modern day televisions in that they come packed with what are essentially eletron cannons to project to their screens, bent by electromagnetic forces to force them to the right position.
